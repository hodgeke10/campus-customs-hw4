# Design Changes

What we changed, and why it helps customers stick around and buy.

| Change | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Collegiate fonts + Yale navy/cream/gold look | Feels like a real, trusted Yale shop, not a template |
| Consistent product cards on clean white, clear price tags | Easy to scan and compare, so shoppers find what they like faster |
| Size picker + Add to Bag + cart with subtotal | A real path to buy in the moment, instead of leaving |
| Sold-out sizes shown but disabled | No dead-end surprises; keeps shoppers confident |
| Real photos on Home and About | Sells the look of the gear and a 50-year shop's credibility |
| Chat starter chips + branded panel | Invites shoppers to search and turns questions into buyable products |
| Subtle motion (hover, fade-in) | Feels polished and maintained |
